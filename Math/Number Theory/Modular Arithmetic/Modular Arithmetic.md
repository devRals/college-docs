"**Modular arithmetic** is a mathematical system that focuses on remainders after division by a chosen positive integer, called the modulus.

## Modulus

The modulus is the number by which we divide to obtain a remainder.

For example, modulo 5 means considering the remainder after division by 5.

## Congruence

The notation:

$$a \equiv b \pmod n$$

means that a and b have the same remainder when divided by n.

Equivalently, n divides a - b.

### Examples

$$17 \equiv 5 \pmod{12}$$

Because 17 and 5 differ by 12.

$$23 \equiv 3 \pmod{10}$$

Because 23 and 3 differ by 20, which is divisible by 10.

## Applications

- Number theory
- Computer science
- Cryptography
- Cyclic patterns
- Time calculations
