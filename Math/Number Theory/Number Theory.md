**Number theory** is a branch of mathematics that studies integers and their properties.

It focuses on understanding relationships between numbers, such as divisibility, factors, prime numbers, and remainders.

## Main topics

- [[Divisibility]] — when an integer divides another integer without a remainder.
- [[Prime Numbers]] — integers greater than 1 with exactly two positive divisors.
- [[Modular Arithmetic]] — arithmetic involving remainders.
- [[Number Systems]] — representations of numbers using different bases.
- [[Greatest Common Divisor]] — the largest integer that divides two or more integers.
- [[Least Common Multiple]] — the smallest positive integer divisible by two or more integers.
